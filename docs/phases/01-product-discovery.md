# Phase 01 — Product Discovery & Scope

## Objective

Define Commerce Order Management Platform as a real commerce/order-management product before writing production code.

## Deliverables

- Problem statement
- Product vision
- Target users: Customer, Admin, Staff
- Primary user journeys
- Business goals
- Assumptions and constraints
- Goals and non-goals
- Success metrics
- Initial risk register
- Initial glossary

## End-to-End Work

1. Describe the customer journey from registration through review.
2. Describe administrative and staff workflows.
3. Identify every business capability required by the specification.
4. Define what Qubrax will explicitly not build in v1.
5. Define measurable success criteria.
6. Record unresolved product questions.
7. Convert decisions into documented artifacts.

## Exit Criteria

- The problem can be explained in 2 minutes.
- Users and their responsibilities are explicit.
- Scope is frozen for the next phases.
- Every major workflow has an owner and success condition.
- No major product ambiguity remains.

You can explain what Qubrax does, for whom, why it exists, and how success will be measured without discussing implementation details.
